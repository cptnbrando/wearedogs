# SEN. SARAH ECKHARDT — ON THE DELTA-9 BAN AND LEGALIZING CANNABIS

Received August 11th 2026, in response to constituent contact about the Texas THC shelf pull and the federal hemp deadline. Reproduced in full, unedited.

We are grateful for the reply. An ally on the record: her office lists decriminalizing, legalizing, and taxing cannabis as a named priority for the 2027 session, and logged the concern in their constituent tracking system.

## THE LETTER

> Thank you for sharing your thoughts about the Delta-9 Ban in Texas. It is an honor to represent the constituents of Senate District 14, and I welcome your opinion about the issues affecting our state and our community.
>
> My goal remains creating a more effective, efficient, fair, and minimally intrusive state government, as we work towards the next legislative session in 2027, I plan to focus the bulk of my time on the following priorities:
>
> Alleviating the housing cost burden for Texans by ensuring an increased supply of affordable housing;
>
> Reducing the burden of the property tax by ensuring that the State fully funds its responsibility to provide high-quality public education;
>
> Improving access to healthcare for all Texans, including mental health services;
>
> Restoring access to the full spectrum of reproductive health care options and protecting those for future generations;
>
> Passing comprehensive criminal justice reforms that focus on the appropriate public safety balance;
>
> Mitigating the effects of climate change by updating our emergency response systems and promoting renewable energy, sustainable practices, and resiliency in our state infrastructure ;
>
> Promoting common-sense gun safety measures;
>
> Supporting the development of a strong Texas economy with the expansion of workforce readiness programs to meet the needs of our evolving economy;
>
> Decriminalizing, legalizing, and taxing cannabis;
>
> Making Texas government more accessible and responsive to the needs of Texans;
>
> Championing the need for more democratic and more accessible Texas elections.
>
> Please know your opinion on this issue is recorded in our constituent tracking system for future reference by my legislative staff to ensure we are accurately representing our constituents' concerns. Your engagement is so important right now, so I encourage you to visit the District 14 website to take action and sign up for my newsletter to stay up to date with all my work under the pink dome this Session.
>
> You can visit https://capitol.texas.gov/Home.aspx to learn more about the legislative process in Texas and track the status of bills. Please do not hesitate to contact me again if I may be of further assistance.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Senator Eckhardt<br>
> Texas Senate, District 14
