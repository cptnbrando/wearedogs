# SEN. TAYLOR REHMET — ON NOT BANNING REGULATED, TAXED, LEGAL PRODUCTS

Received October 2026 from the District 9 office, in response to constituent contact about the Texas THC shelf pull and the federal hemp deadline. Reproduced in full, unedited.

We are grateful for the reply. An ally on the record, in plain words: the office agrees we do not need to be banning products that are regulated, generate taxes, and are legal across the country, and has forwarded the message to the Senator's policy team for review.

## THE LETTER

> My name is Ernesto Gloria, I am the Deputy District Director for Senator Taylor Rehmet. On behalf of Senator Rehmet, thank you for reaching out to our office with your concerns. I am forwarding your message to our policy team to review. I agree that we do not need to be banning products that are regulated, generate taxes, and legal across the country. If you have any additional questions, please feel free to reach back out.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Ernesto Gloria<br>
> Deputy District Director<br>
> Senator Rehmet, D9
