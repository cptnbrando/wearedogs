Thank you for sharing your concerns regarding the impact of the new rules that ban smokeable cannabis and limit THC in Texas.

As you know, regulation on marijuana, hemp, and hemp-derived products like CBD is complex as there are different laws on the state and federal levels. As we’ve seen from the recent changes in hemp product regulations, in addition to legislation, state and federal agencies, judicial action, and orders by the President and the Governor impact the law. There are new legal challenges to the most recent court decision to allow the Texas Department of State Health Services’ rule to take effect. This was not the result of anything on which Senator Campbell voted.

I believe THC has been a focus for legislators in recent history because these products were being aggressively marketed to children. They had also become so prevalent that people didn't know what they were getting, and they could get it anywhere.  Also, despite many people reporting benefits of the products, legislators have heard a great deal of testimony on the negative impacts on often unsuspecting Texans. That's why Senator Campbell, a medical doctor, has supported the safer approach to potential medical benefits of higher levels of THC through the doctor-supervised Texas Compassionate Use Program (TCUP).

I have noted for Senator Campbell's information your support for greater access to cannabis in Texas, so she will know where you stand the next time legislation is considered when the Texas Legislature re-convenes in January.
