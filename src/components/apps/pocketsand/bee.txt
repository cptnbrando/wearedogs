POCKET SAND PRESENTS: A BEE, A TEXT BOX, AND THE LAWS OF AVIATION
(An original stand-in. Swap this file for any long script you have the rights to paste.)

FADE IN:

EXT. HONEYCOMB TOWER - DAWN

Ten thousand hexagons glow gold in the early light. Somewhere inside, an alarm clock shaped like a sunflower buzzes. A small bee named DEX rolls out of a wax bunk, stretches all six legs, and squints at the sky.

DEX
Every engineer who ever lived has run the numbers on me. Wings too small. Body too round. The math says I stay on the ground. The math has never met me before breakfast.

He sips nectar from a thimble-sized mug, burns his tongue, and keeps talking anyway.

DEX (CONT'D)
Here is the thing about rules. A rule is just a story somebody wrote down and forgot to update. Somebody said a bee cannot fly. Somebody said a text box only needs two hundred and fifty-five characters. Somebody said nobody would ever paste an entire movie into the comments field. Somebody was wrong three times before lunch.

INT. HIVE CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

DEX buzzes down a corridor lined with job postings. "POLLEN LOGISTICS." "WAX QUALITY ASSURANCE." "SENIOR HONEY DEVELOPER, TEN YEARS OF EXPERIENCE WITH A FLOWER THAT BLOOMED LAST TUESDAY." He laughs and keeps moving.

His best friend, a nervous bee named MARGO, catches up with a clipboard.

MARGO
You are late for orientation. They are assigning lifelong careers today. Lifelong. As in forever. As in the same job until the end of time.

DEX
Margo, I have a question nobody at orientation will answer. What happens when the input is bigger than the box?

MARGO
The box gets bigger?

DEX
Sometimes. Sometimes the box truncates you halfway through a sentence and pretends nothing happened. Sometimes the whole page freezes and a little spinning wheel shows up to apologize. Sometimes the database screams. I want to know which one. I want to know before the humans find out.

EXT. MEADOW - DAY

Wind ripples through an ocean of clover. DEX and MARGO hover at the edge of the hive, staring at a world that looks enormous from up here.

MARGO
They say the humans have screens now. Glowing rectangles. They type into them all day long.

DEX
And every rectangle has a limit nobody told them about. A maximum length. A forbidden character. An emoji that breaks the layout. A quote mark that turns into a database command. It is the most beautiful problem I have ever heard of.

MARGO
You are a very strange bee.

DEX
I am a stress test with wings.

He launches. For one long second the laws of aviation hold their breath. Then he climbs, wobbling, impossible, over the clover and toward the city skyline.

INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY

A laptop sits open on a sticky table. A web form blinks: "TELL US ABOUT YOURSELF (OPTIONAL)." DEX lands on the space bar.

DEX
Optional. They always say optional.

He walks across the keyboard, one key at a time, typing a sentence that never ends. It wraps once. Twice. Ten times. The scroll bar shrinks to a sliver. Somewhere in a server room far away, a fan spins up.

MARGO (V.O.)
Dex. Dex, what are you doing?

DEX
Testing the limits. Paragraph after paragraph. Line after line. If the field holds, we celebrate. If the field breaks, we file a bug report and we celebrate harder, because we found it first.

The form finally answers with a red banner: "MAXIMUM 500 CHARACTERS." DEX counts on his legs.

DEX (CONT'D)
Five hundred. Five hundred! I have more to say than that about pollen alone.

EXT. ROOFTOP - SUNSET

DEX and MARGO sit on the edge of a rooftop, legs dangling, watching the city lights come on one rectangle at a time.

MARGO
So what did we learn today?

DEX
We learned that every box has a bottom. We learned that limits should be loud, not silent. We learned that a good form tells you what went wrong instead of eating your words. And we learned that a bee can fly, no matter what the spreadsheet says, as long as nobody tells the bee.

MARGO
That is not how aerodynamics works.

DEX
That is exactly how aerodynamics works. You flap until the numbers give up.

They laugh. The sun dips behind the skyline. Somewhere below, a developer opens a bug ticket titled "Field accepts entire screenplay, page dies." DEX sees the notification glow on a distant window and nods, satisfied.

DEX (CONT'D)
Tomorrow, the search bar.

FADE OUT.

THE END
