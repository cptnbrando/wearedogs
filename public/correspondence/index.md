# CORRESPONDENCE

This is where the letters live. What we sent, what came back, and what we said next — printed in full, unedited, with the dates on them.

Replies from officials are reproduced exactly as received. We do not trim the parts we disagree with. If an office took the time to write back with an actual argument in it, that argument gets published whole, and then it gets answered with sources.

**We support anybody and everybody who keeps it real and continues the discussion.** Every party, every district, every position. If you write back with a real reason, you are on the right side of the only line we actually care about. Disagreement is not the problem — a closed door is. Any official who engages honestly, who cites their evidence, who lets the conversation stay open, has our respect and our thanks, whether they end up voting with us or against us. Keep talking to us and we will keep talking to you. That's the whole deal.
