Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · covers wearedogs.net and dogs.red (the same site)

Reading this site sends nothing about you to us. The only feature that does is the like button on the info page. This page explains exactly what happens when you press it.

What happens when you like DOGS

When you click the thumbs-up, and not before:

  1. Your browser saves two small items in its own storage (localStorage, not cookies): a random ID for this browser (wad-device), and the fact that you liked plus the like count (wad-likes).
  2. Your browser reads a few traits of your device to build a device fingerprint: browser name (without version numbers), operating system platform, languages, time zone, screen size and colour depth, CPU core count, memory and touch support, how your graphics hardware describes itself, and how your device draws a small test image and renders a short silent test sound. Each trait is turned into a one-way hash on your device; the raw values never leave it.
  3. Cloudflare Turnstile, an invisible check that you are a person rather than a bot, runs in the background. To do that, Cloudflare processes information such as your IP address and browser details under its Turnstile privacy addendum.
  4. The hashes, the random ID and the Turnstile result are sent to our API (api.wearedogs.net or api.dogs.red), which runs on Cloudflare.

What we store

  • the random ID, hashed again with a secret key
  • your IP address, hashed with a secret key (for IPv6, only the network half: your connection, not your device)
  • each device-trait hash, hashed again with a secret key
  • the time you liked

We never store your raw IP address or raw device information, and without our secret key the stored hashes cannot be turned back into them. Your like itself is just +1 on the count, a number with nothing attached to it.

Why

To count one like per device without making anyone sign up, and to stop bots from inflating the count. A like is treated as a repeat when it comes from the same network as an earlier like and the device traits nearly all match. That is the only use. We do not use any of this to follow you across other sites, build a profile of you, show ads, or sell or share it with anyone. Cloudflare runs the service on our behalf.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by clicking the like button: the notice that appears above it says so, and links here. If you never click it, none of the above happens.

How long we keep it

Your like is forever: the count only ever goes up. The fingerprint record behind it (the hashes and the time) is deleted automatically 20 days after you like. From then on we have no way to recognise your device, so the button opens up again and you can like DOGS again.

Your browser also stops treating you as having liked after 20 days. It keeps its two stored items until you clear them.

Your choices and rights

  • Don't click like, and nothing is collected.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove the two stored items.
  • Because we only keep keyed hashes, we cannot tell which stored record is yours, so we cannot look it up, export it or delete it individually before the 20 days are up. You can still ask us anything about it, or object, using the email us card on the info page.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosts that serve these pages see standard request information such as IP addresses: GitHub Pages for wearedogs.net, and Cloudflare for dogs.red, our media and our API.

Changes

If this changes, this page and its date change with it.
