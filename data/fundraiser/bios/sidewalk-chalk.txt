DEFENDING TEMPORARY PUBLIC ART: SIDEWALK CHALK LEGAL DEFENSE FUND

Sidewalk chalk is non-permanent, non-toxic, eco-friendly, and washes away completely with the first rainstorm. Yet across the country, artists and kids are being targeted, threatened with vandalism charges, harassed, and fined for simply bringing color and positivity to public concrete walkways.

THIS FUNDRAISER SUPPORTS:
1. Legal representation and fine coverage for artists facing bogus vandalism/graffiti citations for temporary chalk drawings.
2. Community sidewalk chalk kits and public art advocacy campaigns to educate local authorities on artistic rights.
3. Rapid-response legal assistance for artists ticketed while making harmless pavement art.

WHY SIDEWALK CHALK IS NOT VANDALISM:
- 100% temporary and water-soluble (cleans off effortlessly with water or rain).
- Causes zero property damage or permanent markings.
- Brings joy, expression, and vibrancy to public spaces.

Support local sidewalk chalk artists and help protect free expression on public walkways!
